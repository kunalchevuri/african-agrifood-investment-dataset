## 1b. Robustness - H1 on restricted deal universes

Two classes of deal could inflate DFI centrality without being
evidence that a DFI drew in an outside investor. First, IFC acts as
implementing entity for both the IDA Private Sector Window and the
GAFSP Private Sector Window, so a syndicate made up only of those
three is one institution drawing on three pots of money. Second,
where the investee is itself a fund or a bank rather than an
agrifood operating company, DFIs appear together as co-limited-
partners or co-lenders, which is a different relation from
co-investing in a firm. H1 is re-run with each class removed.

## 2c. H1 adjusted for participation intensity

Betweenness rises mechanically with degree, and DFIs co-invest more
often than other investors. These four tests ask whether the DFI
advantage survives holding participation constant. They do not fully
agree, and the paper reports all four.

- mean degree: DFI 17.0, non-DFI 7.9 (27 DFIs of 416 nodes)
- raw betweenness ratio: 5.98x

| test | holds constant | result | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| OLS on log degree | degree, linearly | coef +0.00973 | 0.0001 |
| degree-matched pairs | degree, tightly | 8 of 27 DFIs higher | 0.5947 |
| label permutation | nothing (no independence assumption) | null 1.03x | 0.0001 |
| stratified permutation | degree, by quintile | null 2.40x | 0.0060 |
